# TerraPeak
TerraPeak is an idle exploration game where you manage mountains.
You get progressively stronger mountains. 

## Management
To manage the mountains, you need 3 things:
- Wood
- Gas
- Peaches -> Food
Each person consumes 1 wood, 0.1 gas, and 2 peaches per 30 seconds.
You have peach farms, forests, and gas vents.
You have to manage your population. 

## Goal
Your goal is to sustain a population on the mountains and get the highest population possible.

## Progress
The larger population you get, the "better" (taller) mountain you get. Better mountains have more access to resources.
You get an extra explorer every 30 seconds. The second any of your resources drop below 0, you lose.
- 1 Lumberjack gets 1 wood/15 seconds
- 1 Researcher gets 0.25 gas/15 seconds
- 1 farmer gets 2 peaches/15 seconds
Atleast 10% of your population will be civillians 
Civillians cannot do any of the above jobs.
This goes to 10->15->22->30% of population as you go up the peaks

## Resources
Values depend, but resources will be limited by:
50 trees, after chopping down, they regrow in a minute
Infinite gas, but the rate is super slow
Infinite crops, but there's variance
The more crops you have, the less you harvest
"Soil overuse"
So if you have more than 200% of your population in peaches,
so enough for the entire next cycle, 
then, your production drops to 80% -> 50% -> 10%
for 300%->500%->700%


Mountains I know: Mt. fuji, Mt. Everest, Mt. Alishan, Rainier, Kilimanjaro

## Upgrades
To make life sustainable, you can upgrade each branch of workers.
You can also click on a specific branch to make it produce 
10 -> 20 -> 30% more.
You can upgrade each branch of workers or yourself. 
However, your job is to maximize total amount of resources.

All these values are dummy values
